# MacReady

## Register
product

## Product Purpose
MacReady is a macOS-inspired web app for Mac-focused readers. It presents current Mac news, Mac gaming, app discovery, and reviews inside a desktop-style interface.

## Users
Mac users who want a focused, visual way to scan current Mac news and related Mac ecosystem updates without iPhone, iPad, or generic Apple clutter.

## Tone
Native, polished, compact, and useful. The interface should feel like a refined Mac app rather than a marketing site.

## Strategic Principles
- Real data only. Do not use placeholder articles, fake app data, or invented content.
- Keep the macOS desktop metaphor, but make every element serve reading, discovery, or navigation.
- Prefer clean hierarchy over decorative effects.
- Source names, dates, and actions must be readable and consistently placed.
- Mac content comes first: Mac hardware, macOS, Mac apps, Mac gaming, and Mac software compatibility.

## Anti-References
- Placeholder dashboards.
- Generic Apple news feeds dominated by iPhone or iPad content.
- Over-bright blue text in content areas.
- Floating text blocks without clear alignment.
- Decorative controls that do not have a real purpose.
