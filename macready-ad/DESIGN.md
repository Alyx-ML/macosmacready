# MacReady Ad Design

## Style Prompt

Apple-style product film for MacReady, using only real screenshots from the local site. The ad should feel like macOS Tahoe: dark glass, soft focus, precise typography, quiet motion, and polished desktop depth. Keep the tone premium and direct. Show what MacReady is and what it does: Apple news, Mac games, app discovery, wallpaper settings, reviews, utilities, and the built-in assistant.

## Colors

- Background: `#050508`
- Text: `#f5f7fb`
- Muted text: `#b9c2d6`
- System blue: `#007aff`
- Glass purple: `#6b2fd3`
- Glow cyan: `#55d7ff`

## Typography

- Primary: Apple system UI
- Weight range: 300 to 800
- Large, sparse product copy. No long paragraphs.

## Motion

- Soft focus transitions between real screenshots
- Slow screenshot scale and parallax
- Text enters with short, controlled movement
- Final frame fades gently

## What NOT to Do

- Do not fake product screens.
- Do not draw replacement UI.
- Do not use stock imagery.
- Do not use loud neon gaming effects.
- Do not use jump cuts.
